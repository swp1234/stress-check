What this builder is—and is not

It helps organize observable facts, one request, and your own next action. It cannot predict another person’s response and is not legal, medical, workplace, or relationship-safety advice.

Safety comes before a script

Do not use a confrontation script if you fear violence, stalking, coercion, retaliation, or immediate danger. Seek support from a trusted local professional, workplace representative, or emergency service appropriate to your situation.
