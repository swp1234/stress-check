An evidence-based self-reflection tool evaluating perceived autonomic tension and cognitive stress across 5 distinct life domains. Grounded in Sheldon Cohen's Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10) and Hans Selye's General Adaptation Syndrome (GAS), this interactive tool delivers instant multi-axis breakdowns, a private 7-day somatic regulation plan, and an exportable 1080x1920 Instagram Story Card.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **5-Domain Multi-Axis Analysis**: Breaks down stress sources across Work & Study, Somatic Health, Interpersonal Relationships, Financial Strain, and Daily Environment.
- **1080x1920 High-Resolution Story Card**: Generates an obsidian neon-glow diagnosis card for Instagram Stories, Threads, and camera roll via the Web Share API.
- **Private 7-Day Somatic Action Plan**: Delivers tailored nervous system down-regulation protocols (physiological cyclic sighing, progressive muscle relaxation, cognitive boundary scripts).
- **Curated Evidence-Based Literature**: References seminal works (*Burnout* by Dr. Emily Nagoski, *The Highly Sensitive Person* by Dr. Elaine Aron) for continuous recovery.
- **100% Client-Side Privacy**: Zero responses, categories, or scores are ever sent to external servers or telemetry. Everything calculates locally in the browser runtime.
- **12 Global Languages**: English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Russian, Hindi, Indonesian, and Turkish.
