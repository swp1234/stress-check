Why the plan is intentionally small

Stress plans are easier to use when actions are specific and brief. This worksheet combines routine, grounding, movement, connection, and review prompts; it does not diagnose or treat a health condition.

  • WHO: Doing What Matters in Times of Stress
  • CDC: Managing Stress

When a worksheet is not enough

If stress is severe, persistent, affects daily functioning, or you feel unsafe, contact a qualified health professional or local emergency service. This planner is educational self-help, not medical advice.
